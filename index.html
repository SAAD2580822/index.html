Direct vs. Indirect Energy Experiment

Why does tilt change the temperature? Imagine a flashlight shining on a desk. When held straight down, the beam is concentrated. When tilted, the same amount of energy spreads out over a larger area!

Tilt Angle: 0°
Energy Spread Area: Small (Concentrated)
Heat / Temperature Impact: High Energy (Hotter)
Key Takeaway: Summer isn't warm because we are physically closer to the Sun; it is warm because the Sun's rays strike us directly overhead, concentrating solar heat into a smaller surface area!
MYTH

The Popular Myth

"Summer happens because Earth gets physically closer to the Sun during its elliptical orbit, and Winter happens because we pull far away."

❌ False! Earth's orbit is almost a perfect circle. The slight distance variation is far too small to cause giant weather shifts.
FACT

The Scientific Fact

Earth is actually closest to the Sun in early January (Perihelion)—right in the middle of Winter for the Northern Hemisphere!

✔️ Proof: If distance caused seasons, the whole world would experience Summer at the exact same time. Instead, North and South hemispheres always have opposite seasons!
